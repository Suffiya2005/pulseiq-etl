\# Task 3 — Dashboard Style Guide



\## Objective

Create a consistent, readable Headcount \& Departments dashboard for PulseIQ HR Analytics.



\## Colour Palette

\- Primary: Navy blue — `#1F3A5F`

\- Secondary: Teal — `#009E73`

\- Highlight: Orange — `#E69F00`

\- Neutral background: White — `#FFFFFF`

\- Text: Dark grey — `#333333`



Use colour-blind-conscious colours and never rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.



\## Typography

\- Use clear sans-serif fonts.

\- Use large, readable dashboard headings.

\- Keep chart titles and labels consistent.



\## Chart Rules

\- Every chart must have a descriptive title and a one-line takeaway.

\- Use line charts for trends.

\- Use bar charts for department and role comparisons.

\- Display units and date ranges clearly.



\## KPI Order

1\. Current Headcount

2\. Department Count

3\. Monthly Joiners

4\. Monthly Leavers



Use the actual metric definitions from the warehouse metric dictionary.



\## Number Formats

\- Headcount: whole numbers

\- Percentages: percentage with an appropriate precision

\- Dates: consistent year-month format for monthly trends



\## Responsive Layout

\- Show four KPI cards at the top.

\- Place charts below the KPI row.

\- Keep filters accessible on laptop and mobile screens.



\## Design Review

The design must be checked for readability, information hierarchy, accessible colours, and consistent chart titles. Record the actual changes made after reviewing the rendered dashboard; do not claim unverified improvements.



\## Data Integrity

Every KPI and chart must match its warehouse query and metric definition. Takeaway sentences must be written from verified results, not assumed values.
